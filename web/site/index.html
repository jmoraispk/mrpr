Questions

Is Mr. PR an AI code reviewer?+

No. Mr. PR is an inbox for the human reviewer. It works alongside any AI review bots your team already runs.

GitLab and GitHub already have merge request pages. Why this?+

They do, and both are decent. What neither does is put GitLab and GitHub in one list, review in a split pane, or let you mute a merge request until someone actually pushes to it. That is the gap Mr. PR fills.

When is GitHub support coming?+

GitHub is next on the roadmap. Join the waitlist and you'll be the first to know when it's ready.

Does it work with self-managed GitLab?+

Yes. Sign in with your GitLab account. Because Mr. PR talks to GitLab straight from your browser, it works with any instance your browser can reach, even one behind your company VPN.

Why does Mr. PR ask GitLab for full API access?+

Approving, replying and requesting changes need it: GitLab has no narrower permission that allows them. Mr. PR is a static site that acts only when you click: it posts nothing on its own, there is no AI reading your code, and your access token never leaves your browser.

Do you store my source code?+

No. Mr. PR runs in your browser and talks to GitLab directly. Your access token stays on your device, and your code and comments never pass through our servers. Private by design.
