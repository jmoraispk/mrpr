Privacy

Mr. PR is private by design. Here is everything it keeps, and where.

In your browser

  • Your GitLab token stays in your browser. It never reaches our servers.
  • Mr. PR talks to GitLab straight from your browser. Your code, merge requests and comments never pass through our servers.
  • Your settings, what you marked done and what you snoozed live in this browser too.

On our server

  • For each GitLab server, the ID of the sign-in application someone set up for Mr. PR, so the next person from that server can sign in directly.
  • Emails given for the GitHub waitlist.
  • Feedback you send: your message, an email if you give one, the app version and your browser.
  • The first time each person signed in: the GitLab server, the numeric user ID and the date.
  • Your IP address is kept for up to two hours, only to limit abuse of these forms.

Nothing else. To have something deleted, send Feedback from the app with your request.
